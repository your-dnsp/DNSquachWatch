# Corroborate or challenge an observation

Post this information as a comment on the original research issue so its evidence stays together:

- **Result:** supports / contradicts / inconclusive.
- **Equipment:** manufacturer/model and how you verified it.
- **Independence:** your own equipment/test, another source, or the same observation rechecked.
- **Method:** what changed when equipment was powered off/on; repeat observations and relevant radio conditions.
- **Evidence:** a reviewed export, relevant primary-source link, or necessary redacted photo.
- **Address type:** public/universally administered, random/local/private, or unknown.
- **Scope:** one observed device, a tested model, or a proposed prefix-level rule requiring more review.
- **Limits:** alternate explanations and what this test cannot establish.

Do not attach an entire backup, unrelated identifiers, credentials or precise personal locations. Please keep disagreements about evidence constructive. A maintainer can record a confirmed status after review; a comment alone does not modify firmware or establish a prefix-wide identity.
